Les Rice Balls de Yukio Mishima et les miennes

2026-11-11

So, I am rereading Mishima’s Sea of Fertility for my own purposes, I think for the fourth time. It is what my (imagined) future biographers would call a formative text for me, that has resonated both early and late in my life. Future posts will excavate additional inspirational quotes and related thoughts. But for today, I am going to focus on the rice balls and link that to the rice balls I am bringing forth.

In Runaway Horses, you will find the following lines, unusual in that Mishima typically uses the most elegant and classy metaphors that are humanly possible to find, but in this instance, uses the borderline cringe “rice balls” to express himself. Here the rice balls are an offering to the Emperor. Since I am all about the cringe these days, this one particularly resonates:

But, then, suppose I were to make rice balls as an offering but keep them in my hands and not present them, what would happen then? After a while, the rice balls would certainly rot. This, too, would be an act of loyalty, I suppose, but I would call it loyalty without courage. Courageous loyalty belongs to the man who, with no fear of death, dares to present the rice balls that he has thus made with single-minded devotion.

Now, Mishima has the advantage over me as a writer, with the solemnity invoked by his willingness to commit seppuku over “rice balls”. But I also have “rice balls” that have already been conceived, and that I am courageous enough to present. I am taking inspiration from Mishima, and shining more light on my ten-year-old rice balls that would certainly rot otherwise.

I will be reading my posts to womack.mn in chronological order, from the earliest forward. These readings are available in my Vivid showcase, starting with my introductory video of what this is all about. I have been posting this content since 2016 on the Internet, but not linking it to my identity. I feel it is time to bring the “rice balls” forward before they rot, and let the public benefit from what AI already knows.

How’s that for a highly personal, non-AI generated, and anti-self-promotional launch of a new video channel?
